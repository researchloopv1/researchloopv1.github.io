Every model we build teaches the next one how to be better.

Ouroboros is building an AI system that runs its own machine-learning research — proposing changes, testing them, and keeping what works — so the loop of improvement doesn't have to wait for a person to notice what to try next.

Most machine-learning research still runs on a person's intuition: try a change, wait for the run to finish, read the numbers, decide what to try next. That loop is the bottleneck. It caps how fast a team can improve its own models at the rate a handful of researchers can think of ideas and interpret results.

We think the loop itself can be automated — not the judgment, but the tedious middle part of it. A system that reads its own results, proposes the next experiment, and runs it without waiting on a person doesn't just save time. It can try far more ideas than a team ever would, and it gets sharper at choosing good ones the longer it runs.

What we're building

Now
An automated research assistant that optimizes model architecture and training hyperparameters for applied ML teams — turning a week of manual tuning into an overnight run.
Next
Extending the same loop to data selection and evaluation design, so more of the research cycle runs without a person in it.
Always
A person decides what's worth building. The system just runs the loop faster once it's pointed at a problem.
